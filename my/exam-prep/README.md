# Exam prep log — 70% of the grade

The repo ships **10 past final exams with full solutions** (`exam/`) and **4 mock midterms**
(`exam/mock-midterm-exam/`). This is the single most valuable asset in the course, and almost
everyone wastes it by opening it in January.

## Rules that make past papers actually work

1. **Timed and closed-book**, with only your current `my/cheatsheet/cheatsheet.pdf`. An untimed
   paper with the solutions open teaches you almost nothing.
2. **Grade yourself honestly** against the solution, then log below.
3. Every mistake becomes **one line** in the "Things I keep getting wrong" section of the cheat
   sheet. That feedback loop is the whole point.
4. Start in **October**, not December. Early papers will cover material you haven't seen — do
   only the questions on topics already lectured, and revisit the rest later.
